Credits and sources

Workshop content is based on the organizers’ supplied LIVE CVPR 2027 draft proposal. Dates from its timeline are presented as tentative. Workshop acceptance, the assigned day and room, submission arrangements, and final talk titles remain to be determined.

Layout references

The single-column content sections, circular speaker and organizer portraits, and conventional schedule table draw on the academic workshop layout of HiGen. Additional workshop references include Video World Models, World Models in the Loop, and 3D in the Era of World Models. LIVE’s HTML and CSS are independently implemented.

The Proceedings and Non-Proceedings submission tracks are shown side by side, inspired by Video World Models, with LIVE’s light background, typography, and simple column divider. Track descriptions, proposed policies, and tentative dates are based on the supplied LIVE proposal. Submission portals and the Non-Proceedings final-version deadline remain to be determined. The June 20–21 workshop dates and April 23 archival camera-ready deadline are also listed in the official CVPR 2027 workshop call.

Introduction and research scope

The About paragraph reproduces the motivation passage selected by the organizers from the supplied proposal, with PDF line breaks and word wrapping removed. It describes continuous participation in generative worlds, the requirements for effective interaction, and the workshop’s central research question. Three concise goals express the proposal’s directions: generate at interactive speed; responsive interaction with evolving world state; and HCI and Applications.

The About wording follows the supplied proposal verbatim. The workshop references listed above inform the website layout.
